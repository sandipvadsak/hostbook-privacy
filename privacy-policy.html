HostBook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Summary: Your property, booking, customer, inquiry, document, and business records are stored locally on your device. The free version uses Google AdMob, and subscription management uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. Those services may process limited device, advertising, app-usage, diagnostic, and purchase information as described below.

1. About HostBook

HostBook is a local-first booking manager for stays and venues. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Android app.

2. Business data you enter

Names, business details, properties, photos, bookings, customer contact details, payments, inquiries, reminders, preferences, and PIN settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this business data. It leaves the app only when you choose to share or export a file, catalogue, invoice, or backup through another app or service.

3. Advertising

The free version displays ads supplied by Google AdMob. Depending on your location and consent choices, Google may process advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app interactions, ad performance data, device information, and diagnostics to provide, measure, limit, and protect ads. Where required, HostBook asks for consent before requesting ads and provides privacy options in Settings. Pro users are not shown ads.

See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Google Play processes payments. RevenueCat helps the app check and restore Pro entitlement. Google and RevenueCat may process an app user identifier, product and transaction information, subscription status, app/device information, and diagnostics. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Device permissions and files

  • Files/storage: used when you save, import, export, or share PDFs and backups.
  • Photos/files selected by you: used to attach property photos or documents. HostBook only accesses items you select.
  • Phone, maps, messaging, and sharing apps: opened when you deliberately use the related action; their own privacy policies apply.

6. Storage, security, and deletion

Local app data is protected by Android device security and an optional HostBook PIN. You can edit and delete records in the app, export a backup, or delete all app data from Settings. Uninstalling normally removes app-local data, subject to Android backup/restore settings and any files you exported separately.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your business or personal data. Limited data may be processed by Google and RevenueCat for advertising, fraud prevention, diagnostics, purchases, and subscription entitlement as described above. Information you explicitly share is handled by the destination app or recipient you select.

8. Children

HostBook is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18.

9. International processing and your choices

Google and RevenueCat may process information in countries other than yours under their privacy terms. Available choices include AdMob privacy options in HostBook Settings, Android advertising controls, purchasing Pro to remove ads, and deleting local app data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when HostBook or its service providers change. The date above shows the latest revision.
